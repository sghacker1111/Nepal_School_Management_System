# Exam and registration updates — 10 October 2026

## Existing theory-only examinations

Open **Exams → Edit**, enable **Include Internal Exam / Practical Assessment Component**, then save. This is allowed for an unlocked, unpublished terminal, final or practical examination even after theory marks exist. Exam type and result system remain fixed after marks are recorded, and internal assessment cannot be removed afterwards. Reopen locked/published examinations first.

Internal full/pass marks come from the school's class-subject configuration. The addition is transactional and keeps existing theory marks, theory limits, exam subject IDs and enrollment unchanged. A missing internal maximum must be configured rather than invented. Existing genuine internal marks remain intact; the zero generated by theory-only entry becomes unknown until internal assessment is entered. Results remain incomplete and cannot be published before all required marks are present. Unit tests stay theory-only and primary thematic assessment does not gain artificial TH/IN splits.

## Report issue dates

Exam creation/editing has an automatic/manual issue-date default. Automatic mode calculates the current date in `Asia/Kathmandu` each time the report loads. Manual dates must be valid AD calendar dates. Single and bulk report printing also offers a read-only override, including for locked results; changing a print override does not change the saved examination. **Update Preview / Load Report Cards** applies the choice. The print button submits current controls before opening print, so it does not print a stale date. GPA/percentage links retain the selected issue date.

## EMIS registration

**EMIS Registration Number** is separate from the existing **EMIS ID**. It is required for new manual registrations, stored as text (up to 100 characters) to retain leading zeros, and available in student editing, profiles, list/search, report cards and CSV import/export. Existing student records and older CSV imports may remain blank until the number is known; no identifier is invented or copied from EMIS ID.
